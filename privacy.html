Privacy Policy

Plain-English draft, revised 30 October 2026. Subject to solicitor review before commercial launch.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller is IGX Ltd, company number 13057559, registered office 20-22 Wenlock Road, London N1 7GU. Contact: support@instantwins.co.uk. We are registered with the Information Commission (registration number to be published here on confirmation) and pay the data protection fee.

What we collect

Account details you give us (name, email, date of birth, a hashed password), your entries and your balance ledger, postal entry cards, messages you send via the Support centre, and the identity and age checks we run before a first withdrawal. In test mode we collect no payment details.

Why, and the legal basis

To run your account and entries and pay prizes (performance of our contract with you); to verify that you are 18 or over and resident in Great Britain, keep provable records of every draw, and prevent fraud and multiple accounts (legitimate interests, and legal obligation where the law requires it); to publish winners' first names and counties (consent, which you can refuse); and to email you about the site (consent — we do not use the soft opt-in and we do not market to free entrants without a separate opt-in).

Where it lives, and who sees it

Our processors are Supabase (database, EU infrastructure), Vercel (hosting and serverless functions) and our UK bank for deposits and withdrawals; identity checks on first withdrawal are carried out by us from documents you send, and if we appoint a verification provider it will be named here first. Transfers outside the UK; transfers outside the UK rely on the UK adequacy regulations for the EEA and, where needed, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement. Our independent auditor may see draw and postal-entry logs. We do not sell your data and we run no third-party advertising.

Cookies and tracking

We store up to two items in your browser (local storage, not cookies). The first is your sign-in session, which is strictly necessary to run your account and needs no consent. The second, only if a referral code is in the address you arrived with, is that code, stored for 30 days so we can pay the partner who referred you; this is affiliate tracking and is only stored with your consent, which we ask for on arrival and which you can refuse without losing anything. We set no analytics or advertising cookies.

How long

Account records: while your account is open and for 6 years after it closes (tax and dispute records). Draw results, seeds and postal-entry logs: 6 years, so results stay verifiable. Support messages: 2 years. Identity-check documents: deleted within 30 days of a successful check; the result is kept.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests, at any time by email or via the Support centre; we reply within one month. You can complain to the Information Commission (ico.org.uk) if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.
