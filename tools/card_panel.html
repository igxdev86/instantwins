Card payments (Nochex)

Nochex hosted payment page. Vercel env: NOCHEX_MERCHANT_ID (your Nochex merchant id), NOCHEX_TEST=1 while testing (remove to go live). In Nochex → Payment Page Setup: Call back URL https://instantwins.co.uk/api/nochex, Success URL https://instantwins.co.uk/account.html?pay=ok, Cancel URL https://instantwins.co.uk/account.html?pay=cancelled. Every payment is verified with Nochex before it is credited.
