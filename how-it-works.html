How InstantWins works

The whole site is one simple thing: a round of two million £1 tickets with £1,020,000 of prizes already printed on them. You buy tickets, you reveal them, and whatever is on them is yours. Here is the full picture.

1. One round, two million tickets

Tickets are numbered 1 to 2,000,000 and sold in order. Before the round opens, a secret seed decides which ticket numbers carry which prizes — 351,225 of them, from 50p to £100,000 — and we publish the seed’s SHA-256 hash so it cannot be changed afterwards. When you buy, you get the next tickets in line. Nobody, including us, picks which ticket you get.

2. Buy

Tickets are £1, and the more you buy at once the less each one costs (down to 68p each for 1,000). You pay from your account balance, which you top up by debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank transfer. No credit cards. Prices include VAT. You can set a daily and a monthly spending limit (including £0) or take a break at any time from your account page.

3. Reveal

Your unopened tickets sit in your hopper. Tap Drop one to open a single ticket or Drop all to open the lot — each ticket drops as a ball: grey for nothing, lime with the amount for a prize. The drop is only a way of showing you what is already on the ticket. Nothing you do, and nothing about timing, changes it.

4. Win

A cash prize is credited to your balance the instant the ticket is opened. Withdraw any amount, any time, to the card you paid with or your bank account — normally within one to three working days, no fees, no minimum. Prizes of £100 or more are listed on the winners page with your first name and county, unless you switch that off.

5. The End Prize Draw

Every round has a closing date, shown on the home page. When the round closes — at that date, or sooner if every ticket sells — one End Prize (£10,000 unless the home page says otherwise) is drawn from every ticket issued in the round, winning or blank, paid or free. The draw number comes from the same seed, so you can check it. Unopened tickets are opened for you, the seed is published, and the next round opens.

6. Free entry by post

Every round can be entered free with an unenclosed postcard. A postal ticket is exactly the same as a paid one: same sequence, same odds, same End Prize Draw. Full instructions on the free entry page.

7. Check it yourself

While a round is open you can see the seed hash, how many tickets have sold and how many of each prize are still in. When it closes we publish the seed; anyone can rebuild the entire allocation from it and compare the hash. The method is on the fairness page.

8. The odds, honestly

About one ticket in five carries a prize, and most prizes are small. Across the whole round, 51p of every £1 goes back out as prizes; the rest runs the site. Prizes are a matter of luck. Please only play with money you would otherwise spend on entertainment, and use the limits in your account — see responsible play.

Each round is a prize competition with a free postal entry route under Schedule 2 of the Gambling Act 2005; it is not a lottery and not a casino. Open to Great Britain residents aged 18 or over.
