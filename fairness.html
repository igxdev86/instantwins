Fairness you can check yourself

Our claim is simple: the outcome is never in the game. Every result is fixed and mathematically sealed before you can enter, and everything you need to verify that is published. You don't have to trust us — that's the point.

How the instant games work

1
Before a pool opens, we generate a secret seed and build the pool's entire entry→prize map from it. We publish the seed's SHA-256 fingerprint on the game page while the pool is live.
2
When you buy, you get the next entry number. Its prize was already sitting in the sealed map. The plinko ball, wheel, claw or cards just act it out.
3
The moment the pool sells out, the seed is revealed in the fairness panel. Any pool still open 28 days after opening settles then, with unsold-entry prizes drawn among its entrants.

Verify a pool — the recipe

Check the seal: take the revealed seed, run it through any SHA-256 tool, and confirm the output matches the fingerprint that was displayed while the pool was live. If it matches, that exact seed existed before entries opened — it couldn't have been swapped without breaking the hash.

Rebuild the map (advanced): the shuffle itself is derived from the seed, so you can reconstruct the entire prize map:

1. List the prize fund as positions 1..500 (winners first, then blanks)
2. For each position i, compute  md5(seed + ":" + i)
3. Sort positions by those md5 values — that order IS the entry order
4. The prize at sorted-rank N is what entry #N was always going to get

If our published results ever disagreed with your reconstruction, the revealed seed would prove it.

How the Card draws work

Each Five-Minute Draw and Hourly Grand seals a seed and publishes its SHA-256 fingerprint before entries open. At the off, the winner is derived from the seed plus the final entry count — not picked:

winning entry = ( first 8 hex chars of md5(seed + ":" + entries) as a 32-bit number,
  made positive, modulo entries ) + 1

The seed is revealed at settlement. Re-run the formula yourself with any md5 tool and you'll land on the published winning number. Because the entry count isn't final until the off, not even we know the winner in advance — and because the seed is sealed, we can't steer it afterwards.

What this means

We physically cannot favour an entry, dodge a big prize, or rewrite a result — any of those would break a fingerprint that was on public display while you played. The games are theatre; the maths is the referee. Questions about any specific pool or draw? Ask in the Support centre and we'll walk you through verifying it.
