How Pick a Piece works

Pick a Piece is a provably fair prize puzzle that runs on hourly boards. Each board is a picture divided into 48 pieces. Pick any free piece for £2 — or free by post — and it uncovers that fragment of the hidden image. Before a board opens we commit one winning piece and publish its SHA-256 hash, so the result is fixed in advance and can be verified when the seed is revealed at the close.

Winning and the rolling jackpot

At the top of the hour the board closes and the committed winning piece is unsealed. Whoever holds it wins the jackpot. If that piece was never picked, there is no winner and the jackpot rolls into the next board, growing by £100 each time. Once the jackpot reaches £1,000 it must drop: if the committed piece is unsold, the winner is drawn from the pieces that were sold.

Free entry

You can always enter free by post with no purchase necessary, under Schedule 2 of the Gambling Act 2005. Free and paid pieces have exactly the same chance of being the winning piece.
