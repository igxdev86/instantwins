The method

1. Before the round opens we create a secret seed and publish its SHA-256 hash: —

2. Every ticket number 1–2,000,000 is ranked by MD5(seed:number). The first 411,128 in that ranking are the winning tickets, prizes handed down the ranking largest first (1 × £100,000, 2 × £25,000 … 150,000 × £1, 100,000 × 50p).

3. You get the next ticket numbers in sequence when you buy or post. Revealing a ticket in a game only shows you what that number was always holding.

4. When the last ticket goes, the seed is published. Anyone can recompute the whole ranking and check every prize was where we said.

The End Prize Draw

Every round has a closing date, shown on the home page. At the earlier of sell-out and that date the round closes and one End Prize is drawn from every ticket issued in the round — paid and postal, winning and blank alike. The number is fixed by the same secret seed: 1 + (first 8 hex digits of md5(seed + “:end”)) mod tickets issued. When the seed is published you can recompute it. Any ticket still unopened at close is opened automatically and its instant prize credited. Instant prizes on tickets that were never sold are not awarded.

Games are reveals

Coin Drop, High Striker, Scratchcard, Treasure Chest, Lucky Dip, Bingo and Prize Wheel are five different ways of looking at the same ticket. The same ticket shows the same prize in every one. The pictures are honest: a prize lands only on its own value, a blank only on a blank; there are no near-misses.
