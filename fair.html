The method

1. Before the round opens we create a secret seed and publish its SHA-256 hash: —

2. Every ticket number 1–2,000,000 is ranked by MD5(seed:number). The first 361,128 in that ranking are the winning tickets, prizes handed down the ranking largest first (1 × £100,000, 2 × £25,000 … 200,000 × £1).

3. You get the next ticket numbers in sequence when you buy or post. Revealing a ticket in a game only shows you what that number was always holding.

4. When the last ticket goes, the seed is published. Anyone can recompute the whole ranking and check every prize was where we said.

Games are reveals

Coin Drop, High Striker, Scratchcard, Treasure Chest, Lucky Dip, Bingo and Prize Wheel are five different ways of looking at the same ticket. The same ticket shows the same prize in every one. The pictures are honest: a prize lands only on its own value, a blank only on a blank; there are no near-misses.
