What you’re checking, in plain English

Before anyone bought a number, we wrote down a secret code (the seed) and published its fingerprint — a scrambled version that can’t be turned back into the seed. When the draw closed we revealed the seed. If the revealed seed matches the fingerprint, the seed wasn’t swapped after people entered. Then the winning number comes out of the seed and the number of entries by a fixed sum — not a pick. Your browser does both checks below, live; nothing is taken on trust from us.

Step 2 — Does the winning number come out of the sum?

The sum is: take the seed, add a colon and the number of entries, run it through MD5, take the first 8 characters as a number, keep it positive, divide by the number of entries and keep the remainder, add 1.

Check it somewhere else
