How it works. There is one prize round for the whole site. Every ticket number has its prize assigned before the round opens (seed hash published). When you buy, you get the next tickets in sequence — they sit in your bank until you open them. The games are reveals: a coin drop, a hammer, a scratch panel, a chest, or just the ticket. Nothing you do in a game changes what the ticket holds, and the same ticket would show the same prize in any game. Free entry by post on exactly the same terms. Prototype: prizes here are simulated in your browser; the live build allocates on the server.
