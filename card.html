ENTER FREE BY POST

Send an unenclosed postcard with your full name, address, date of birth and the email on your InstantWins account to:

InstantWins Free Entry
IGX Ltd, PO Box (confirmed at launch)
United Kingdom

Name the draw you want — e.g. “Five-Minute Draw, Tue 3 Nov, 19:05” or “Hourly Grand, Sat 7 Nov, 20:00”. Any draw in the next 7 days can be named; allow 3 working days for second-class post. If you name no draw, your card enters the next draw after it is processed and every Five-Minute Draw for the rest of that day.

Postal entries take the next number in the same sequence as paid entries — same draw, same odds, same prize. One entry per postcard; send as many as you like. Postcards are processed the working day they arrive. No purchase necessary.
