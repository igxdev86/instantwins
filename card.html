The Card is part of the single continuous arrangement of free-entry prize draws on InstantWins.co.uk, structured under the free-entry provisions of Schedule 2 of the Gambling Act 2005 — it is not gambling and no licence is required. Every draw may be entered free by post with identical odds; free entries are processed ahead of paid sales.
