ENTER FREE BY POST

Send an unenclosed postcard with your full name, your account number (starts IW — see your Account page), your address and date of birth to:

InstantWins Free Entry
IGX Ltd, PO Box (confirmed at launch)
United Kingdom

Write the game number of the draw you want — it’s shown on every draw, e.g. F100-031126-1905 (that’s the £1 Five-Minute draw on 3 Nov at 19:05). Any draw in the next 7 days is open; allow 3 working days for second-class post. If you name no draw, your card enters the next draw after it is processed and every Five-Minute Draw for the rest of that day.

Postal entries take the next number in the same sequence as paid entries — same draw, same odds, same prize. One entry per postcard; send as many as you like. Postcards are processed the working day they arrive. No purchase necessary.
