2. What this site is

Every competition on this site is a prize competition with a free postal entry route, run so as to meet paragraph 8 of Schedule 2 to the Gambling Act 2005: each competition can be entered by paying or, at the entrant's choice, by ordinary post; the free route is publicised with equal prominence; and the prize allocation does not differentiate between paid and postal entries. 5. The competitions

The Million Round. One prize competition of 2,000,000 tickets at £1 each (less per ticket when bought in the quantities shown on the site) carrying the published prize table totalling £1,020,000. Before the round opens, every prize is attached to a ticket number by the published-seed method on the Fairness page, and the seed hash is published. When you buy you receive the next unsold ticket numbers in sequence. Your tickets sit in your account until you open them; the games on the site are different ways of opening a ticket and nothing you do in a game changes the prize on it. Cash prizes are credited the moment a ticket is opened. Every round has a closing date and time, shown on the home page. The round closes at the earlier of every ticket being taken and that closing time. When it closes: (a) one End Prize of the amount shown on the home page (£10,000 unless stated otherwise) is drawn from every ticket issued in the round, paid and postal alike, by the published-seed method on the Fairness page; (b) any ticket still unopened is opened automatically and its instant prize credited; (c) the seed is published so the whole allocation and the End Prize number can be checked; (d) the next round opens. Instant prizes on tickets that were never sold are not awarded. A closing date is never moved and the End Prize is always drawn and paid, whatever the level of sales.

Entries are final once a ticket has been issued to your account. If a round has to be cancelled for a verified technical fault before it closes, every ticket in it — paid and postal alike — is carried into the replacement round keeping its number, entrants are told by email, and anyone who would rather not take part can have paid tickets refunded.

6. Free entry

The Million Round can be entered free by unenclosed postcard carrying the entrant's full name, InstantWins account number, address, date of birth and the game number of the draw, as set out on the Free entry page, which forms part of these terms. One card is one entry. Postal entries take the next number in the same sequence as paid entries, in the draw (by game number), Instant Win ticket (by its name and price as shown on its page, e.g. “Diamond Ticket £1”) or competition (by its code, with your answer) named on the card. A card naming nothing enters the next open Five Minute draw after it is processed. Cards are processed on the working day they arrive; a card for a draw that has closed or filled goes into the next draw of the same type, exactly as an online entry would. Every live product can be entered this way. No purchase necessary.

6a. Payments

Ticket prices include VAT at the standard rate. Paid entries are bought with credit on your account. You can add credit by UK debit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through our payment processor, or by bank transfer. We do not accept credit cards for any payment, and we never store your card number — it goes to the processor only. Credit added by card is available at once. Your balance is held in a client account separate from IGX Ltd’s own money, is not interest-bearing, and can be withdrawn at any time, in full, with no minimum. A card or wallet payment that is later reversed by the issuer (a chargeback) is treated as a debt on the account; tickets bought with it are void unless already opened, and the account is suspended until it is settled. There is no fee to deposit or withdraw.

7. Prizes and withdrawals

Prizes are paid in cash to your account balance and may be withdrawn at any time, in full and with no minimum, to the debit card you paid with, or by bank transfer to a UK account in your name, normally within 1–3 working days of a request (same day for card refunds where the processor allows), subject to age and identity verification on the first withdrawal. Prize money is never converted to credit that can only be played, and no prize is substituted. Prizes of £100 or more are published on the Results page with the winner's first name and county, which you may decline by telling us.

Because a ticket or draw number is delivered to you instantly, by asking us to issue it you request immediate performance and acknowledge that you lose the right to cancel that entry under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 once it has been issued. Credit you have added to your account but not used can be withdrawn at any time.
