5. The competitions

Draws. Separate timed draws, each with one fixed entry price, one fixed prize and a maximum number of entries, running every 30 seconds to once a day. The draws running at any time, their prices, prizes and maximums are those shown on the Draws page, which forms part of these terms; we may add, pause or remove a draw row, which never affects entries already placed. Every draw is open for entry seven days ahead and closes at its draw time. Draws are one of two types, stated on the draw’s page. Rollover draws (most paid draws): the winning number is drawn from every number in the draw (for example 1 to 40). If the number drawn has been taken, that entrant wins the full prize plus any rollover. If it has not been taken, nobody wins that draw and the whole prize rolls into the next draw of the same type, where it is shown as a rollover; it keeps rolling until a taken number is drawn, so every pound of prize money is eventually paid out. The chance of a winner in any draw is the number of entries divided by the numbers in the draw, and is shown on the tile. Entrants-only draws (all free draws): the winner is drawn from the numbers actually entered, so there is always a winner. A draw of either type with no entries is void: nothing is taken and nothing is carried over. Some draws offer a bundle of one number in each of the next several draws at a stated price; a bundle that cannot be fully placed is refunded pro rata. Free draws cost nothing and allow one entry per account per draw.

The Million Round. One prize competition of 2,000,000 tickets at £1 each (less in a pack, as shown on the home page) carrying the published prize table totalling £1,020,000. Before the round opens, every prize is attached to a ticket number by the published-seed method on the Fairness page, and the seed hash is published. When you buy you receive the next unsold ticket numbers in sequence. Your tickets sit in your account until you open them; the games on the site are different ways of opening a ticket and nothing you do in a game changes the prize on it. Cash prizes are credited the moment a ticket is opened. The round stays open until every ticket is taken, so every prize in the table is paid to a ticket holder. Unopened tickets are opened automatically when the round closes and any prize credited.

Instant Win tickets. Each ticket type (shown on the Instant Wins page with its name, price and prize table, e.g. “Diamond Ticket £1”) runs in rounds of a fixed number of tickets. The round’s complete prize table is published before it opens and every prize in the table is attached to a ticket in that round. A round stays open until every ticket is taken, so every listed prize is paid to a ticket holder. Your ticket shows its prize at the moment it is issued; cash prizes are credited at once. Item prizes. Some rounds carry a physical item (shown with its RRP and a cash alternative) on one or more tickets. The ticket that carries it is credited with the cash alternative immediately; the winner may instead request the item itself within 7 days through their account, in which case the cash alternative is reversed and the item is delivered, insured and signed-for, to a Great Britain address within 14 days. Items are new unless stated, bought at retail or from an authenticated reseller, and the purchase receipt is shown to the winner.

Competitions. A single prize with a qualifying question and a short list of answers, open for a stated period (normally seven days). Entry is online for the stated fee or free by post. When the competition closes, one winner is drawn at random from all correct entries, paid and postal alike, by the published-seed method described on the Fairness page, and the prize is credited to the winner's account. Incorrect answers are not entered into the draw and paid incorrect entries are not refunded. If there are no correct entries the prize is not awarded. The question is a qualifying question and does not make the competition a skill competition; the free postal route is what keeps it outside the Gambling Act 2005.

Entries are final once your ticket has been shown or your number issued. A draw that has not yet closed can be cancelled by us only for a verified technical fault, in which case every entry in it — paid and postal alike — is carried into the replacement draw we announce (normally the next draw of the same type), keeping its number, and entrants are told by email. Anyone who would rather not take part in the replacement can ask for a refund of a paid entry.

6. Free entry

Every draw can be entered free by unenclosed postcard carrying the entrant's full name, InstantWins account number, address, date of birth and the game number of the draw, as set out on the Free entry page, which forms part of these terms. One card is one entry. Postal entries take the next number in the same sequence as paid entries, in the draw (by game number), Instant Win ticket (by its name and price as shown on its page, e.g. “Diamond Ticket £1”) or competition (by its code, with your answer) named on the card. A card naming nothing enters the next open Five Minute draw after it is processed. Cards are processed on the working day they arrive; a card for a draw that has closed or filled goes into the next draw of the same type, exactly as an online entry would. Every live product can be entered this way. No purchase necessary.

6a. Payments

Paid entries are bought with credit on your account. You can add credit by UK debit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through our payment processor, or by bank transfer. We do not accept credit cards, and we never store your card number — it goes to the processor only. Credit added by card is available at once. Your balance is held in a client account separate from IGX Ltd’s own money, is not interest-bearing, and can be withdrawn at any time, in full, with no minimum. A card or wallet payment that is later reversed by the issuer (a chargeback) is treated as a debt on the account; tickets bought with it are void unless already opened, and the account is suspended until it is settled. There is no fee to deposit or withdraw.

7. Prizes and withdrawals

Prizes are paid in cash to your account balance and may be withdrawn at any time, in full and with no minimum, to the debit card you paid with, or by bank transfer to a UK account in your name, normally within 1–3 working days of a request (same day for card refunds where the processor allows), subject to age and identity verification on the first withdrawal. Prize money is never converted to credit that can only be played, and no prize is substituted. Prizes of £100 or more are published on the Results page with the winner's first name and county, which you may decline by telling us.

Credit you have added to your account but not used can be withdrawn at any time.
