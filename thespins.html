The Spins — 40 Online Slot Games, One Prize Round

The Spins is InstantWins.co.uk’s slot suite: forty themed games — from Fruit Frenzy and Egypt Gold to Dragon’s Hoard, Wolf Moon and Book of Secrets — all feeding a single shared prize fund. Every spin, on any theme, takes the next numbered entry from the same sealed £10,000 allocation, so the odds are identical whichever slot you choose.

Classic & Deluxe slots

The twenty Classics are straight three-reel games: line up three symbols to win. The twenty Deluxe slots add feature theatre — free spins, hold & spin, cascades, multipliers, expanding wilds, respins and bonus wheels — all of which are presentation layered over the same provably-fair draw. The feature you trigger is how your sealed prize is revealed; it never changes the result.

Why one shared prize fund

Forty games feeding one round means it fills fast, pays often and carries a big headline prize. One shared £10,000 round would be forty near-empty rounds if split up — instead you get constant winners, a £1,000 top prize on every theme, and provably identical odds across all forty slots.

Odds & prizes

Each round is 5,000 spins at £2 with a £5,000 prize table: a £1,000 top prize, then £100, £50, £25, £10, £5 and £2 tiers — 786 winners in all, about a 1-in-6 chance per spin. The full table resets every time a round sells out, so the odds never change.

Provably fair

Before a round opens, its entire spin-to-prize allocation is shuffled, fixed and published as a SHA-256 fingerprint. When the round sells out the seed is revealed, so anyone can rebuild the allocation and confirm The Spins paid exactly what was sealed.
