How Instant Wins work

The round is printed before it opens. For each round we fix a secret seed, build the ticket→prize allocation from it, and publish the SHA-256 of the seed on the round page. Nothing about your ticket is decided when you buy it — it was decided, and committed to, before the round opened.

You see exactly what you bought. Your ticket shows its number and the prize attached to it. There is no spin, drop or pick — the page simply shows you the ticket.

The seed is published at sell-out so anyone can rebuild the round and confirm every prize in the table went to the ticket it was attached to. Every prize in every round is paid.

Free entry, always. Name the round (e.g. “Diamond Ticket”) on a postcard with your name and account number and your entry takes the next ticket in the same sequence as paid entries. See the Free entry page.
