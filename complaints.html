Complaints

If something has gone wrong, tell us. This is the procedure we follow, in full, and it costs you nothing.

How to complain

1
Email support@instantwins.co.uk with "Complaint" in the subject, or message us from the Support centre. Say what happened, when, and what you would like us to do. Include your account number if you have one.
2
We acknowledge within 2 working days and give you a reference.
3
We investigate and give you a full written answer within 10 working days. For anything about a draw or a round we include the seed, commitment and entry count so you can check the result yourself.
4
If you are not satisfied, say so and the complaint is reviewed again by the director, with a final answer within a further 10 working days.

If we still disagree

You can take the matter to your local Citizens Advice consumer service (0808 223 1133), raise it with Trading Standards, or bring a claim in court. Complaints about an advertisement can go to the Advertising Standards Authority (asa.org.uk). Complaints about your personal data can go to the Information Commission (ico.org.uk). Nothing in this procedure removes any right you have in law.

Our record

We keep a log of every complaint, what it was about and how it was resolved, and make it available to our independent auditor. We publish the number received and upheld each year on this page.
