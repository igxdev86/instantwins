How Beat the Board works

Beat the Board is a participation prize quiz in the classic call-TV style. Each round poses one question with many correct answers — and a board of sixteen hidden slots holding them. Submit a guess for £1, or free by post; if it matches an answer on the board, that slot is revealed and claimed. One slot is the committed golden answer, worth the jackpot.

The golden answer and the full board are hashed before the round opens and published afterwards, so the result is provably fair and fixed in advance. If nobody guesses the golden answer, the jackpot rolls into the next board. Free postal entry carries the same chance of landing the golden answer. 18+, Schedule 2 of the Gambling Act 2005.
