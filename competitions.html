How competitions work

One prize, one question. Every competition shows the prize, the entry fee, the question and its answers before you enter. The question is a qualifying question — only correct entries go into the draw.

Enter online or free by post. Online entry costs the fee shown and is taken from your credit. Free entry: send a postcard with the competition code (e.g. C001), your answer, your name and your InstantWins account number to the address on the Free entry page. Postal entries are keyed in as they arrive, must reach us before the close, and have exactly the same chance as paid entries.

The draw. When the competition closes, one entry is picked at random from all the correct entries using a seed we committed to before it opened (its SHA-256 is shown on the competition). The seed is published with the result so anyone can check the pick on the Fairness page. The winner is credited automatically and shown on the results list by first name.

If nobody gets it right the prize is not awarded and paid entries are not refunded — so do read the question. We keep the questions simple on purpose.
