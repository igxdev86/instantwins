How Pick a Piece works

Pick a Piece is a provably fair prize puzzle. A single picture is divided into a grid of 48 pieces. Each entry claims one numbered piece for £2, or free by post, and uncovers that fragment of the hidden image. Before the board opens we draw one winning piece and publish its SHA-256 commitment hash, so the result is fixed in advance and verifiable — we can’t change it once play starts.

Winning

When all 48 pieces are claimed the board completes, the full picture is revealed, and the committed winning piece is unsealed. Whoever holds that piece wins the £500 prize. If the winning piece was never claimed in that round, there is no winner and the prize rolls over — growing the pot for the next board.

Free entry

You can always enter free by post with no purchase necessary, under Schedule 2 of the Gambling Act 2005. Free and paid entries have exactly the same chance of holding the winning piece.

Is it fair?

Yes. The winning piece is committed as a hash before any piece is sold, and the seed is published afterwards so anyone can confirm the draw was never altered. This is the same provably-fair method used across InstantWins draws.
