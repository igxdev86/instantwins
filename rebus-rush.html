How Rebus Rush works

Rebus Rush is a skill-based prize puzzle. Each round shows five rebus puzzles — pictures and symbols that spell out a word or title. Solve all five correctly to qualify. Because many players can solve them, a committed estimation tiebreaker decides the single winner, so skill determines the outcome under Schedule 2 of the Gambling Act 2005.

Entry is £1, or free by post with no purchase necessary. Answers and the tiebreaker are hashed before the round opens and revealed afterwards, so every result is verifiable.
