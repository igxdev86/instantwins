How games work

A game is a reveal, not a chance. When you buy an Instant Win ticket, the prize on it was decided before the round opened and is printed on the ticket number you get. The game simply shows it to you — a hammer, a scratch panel or a chest lid instead of plain text. Nothing you tap, scratch, time or hit changes the result, and we say so on every game.

Honest pictures. The High Striker ball stops at your prize’s real mark and rings the bell only on the top prize; a losing ticket hits low. The chest glows only when there is a prize. There are no near-misses.

Same tickets, same odds. Every game uses the ordinary ticket rounds shown on the Instant Wins page, with the published prize table and seed hash, and every round can be entered free by post. You can switch to the plain reveal at any time.
