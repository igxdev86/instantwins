How ticket reveals work

The book is printed before it opens. For each round the operator fixes a secret seed, builds the ticket→prize allocation from it, and publishes the SHA-256 of the seed on the round page. Nothing about your ticket is decided when you buy it — it was decided, and committed to, before the round opened.

You see exactly what you bought. Your ticket shows its number and the prize attached to it. There is no spin, drop, pick or reveal mechanic to play — the page simply shows you the ticket.

The seed is published at sell-out. When the last ticket sells the seed is revealed so anyone can rebuild the allocation and confirm every prize in the table was in the book and went to the ticket it was attached to. Every prize in every book is paid.

Free entry, always. Every round can be entered free by post — name the ticket book on your postcard and your entry takes the next ticket in the same sequence as paid entries, with the same chance of every prize. See the Free entry page.
