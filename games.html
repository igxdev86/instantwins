How the games work

The outcome is never in the game. Before each pool opens, a shuffled entry→prize allocation is fixed and its SHA-256 hash is published on the game page. Buying an entry reveals the prize already attached to your entry number — the plinko ball, the claw, the rocket are presentation only and change nothing.

The seed is revealed at sell-out. The moment a pool's final entry sells, its seed is published so anyone can re-hash it and confirm it matches the commitment shown while the pool was live.A pool has no time limit — it stays open until every one of its numbered entries has sold, and only then does it settle and reveal its seed.

Free entry, always. Every pool of every game can be entered free by post with identical odds — free entries are processed ahead of paid sales. See the Free entry page.

All games form part of the single continuous arrangement of free-entry prize draws on InstantWins.co.uk, structured under the free-entry provisions of Schedule 2 of the Gambling Act 2005. No purchase necessary: see Free entry. Prize tables and entry caps are published per pool and never change mid-pool. Structure subject to solicitor sign-off before launch. 18+. UK residents only.
