Withdrawals to pay

Pay by bank transfer, then mark paid. Declining refunds the hold to the player.

Million Round postcard

Card names “Million Round” with the entrant's name and account number. The entry takes the next ticket in the round, same sequence as paid, and is opened at once — any prize is credited to their account and they are emailed.

Key in a postcard

Enter the card exactly as written. The entry takes the next number in the named draw or round, in the same sequence as paid entries. Unnamed cards go into the next Five Minute Draw and every Five Minute Draw for the rest of today. Log every card, including rejected ones.

No game number on the card? Use the draw + time fields below, or leave the time blank for “next draw and every Five Minute £100 draw today”.

Instant Win postcard

The card names the ticket exactly as the ticket page tells them to write it — e.g. “Diamond Ticket £1”. Pick that line below. The entry takes the next ticket in that ticket's open round, same sequence as paid, and any prize is credited at once.

Key in a postcard

Postcard must carry the competition code, the answer letter, the entrant's name and their IW account number (email also works). A wrong answer is still recorded but will not be in the draw.

Create a competition

Keep the question easy — it is a qualifying question, not a skill test. The free postal route is what makes this lawful, so never close one early or turn the postal route off.

Turn draws on and off

Switch a whole cadence row or a single draw. Off = no new draws are scheduled and no new entries are taken (online or postal). Draws that already hold entries still run and pay. Takes effect within a few seconds.
